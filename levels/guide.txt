// Spoken by the guide the first time the player dies, and never again.
name: The Guide
Peace, traveler. Do not be afraid.
You have died, but your journey is not over. This is Purgatory, the mountain where souls are made clean.
Seven terraces rise above us, one for each of the seven deadly sins. Climb them, and at the summit your burden will be lifted and the way back will open.
If you cannot wait, the toll gate will return you at once. But nothing here is free. Those who take it carry a burden, and the burdened cannot enter Heaven.
Choose as you will. We will not meet again.
