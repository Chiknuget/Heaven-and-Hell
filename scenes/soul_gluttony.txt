// The Sixth Terrace: a soul who only ever needed one more taste.

if freed_gluttony goto thanks
if met_gluttony goto again
set met_gluttony 1
say The Hungry One: The fruit on that tree. Can you smell it? If I could only have one more taste, I would be satisfied.

label again
say The Hungry One: Just one more. That's all I ever needed. Just one more.
choice I'll reach it for you. = reach | Was one more ever enough? = right | Then go and take it. = take

label reach
say The Hungry One: Yes! And then one more after that, and then...
end

label take
say The Hungry One: I can't reach it! I've never been able to reach it!
end

label right
say The Hungry One: ...No. Never once.
say The Hungry One: I was never hungry for the fruit. I was hungry for the wanting.
say The Hungry One: I think I can leave it on the branch.
set freed_gluttony 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The Hungry One: I haven't thought about food since I got here. I didn't know that was possible.
end
