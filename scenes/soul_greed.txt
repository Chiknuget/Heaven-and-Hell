// The Fifth Terrace: a soul who will climb as soon as he has enough.

if freed_greed goto thanks
if met_greed goto again
set met_greed 1
say The Counter: Nine hundred ninety-eight. Nine hundred ninety-nine. Don't touch them!
say The Counter: When I reach a thousand, I'll have enough. Then I'll climb.

label again
say The Counter: I lost count again. One. Two. Three...
choice I'll help you count. = help | Will a thousand be enough? = right | You can't carry it up there. = carry

label help
say The Counter: Help? You'll steal them! Get away!
end

label carry
say The Counter: Then I won't go up there!
end

label right
say The Counter: ...No. It never was. Not at a hundred, not at ten thousand.
say The Counter: There was always one more coin between me and enough.
say The Counter: I don't think I need to count anymore.
set freed_greed 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The Counter: My hands feel so light. I keep reaching for my pockets out of habit.
end
