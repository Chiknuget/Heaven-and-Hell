// The Second Terrace: a woman who can't stop watching the souls above her.

if freed_envy goto thanks
if met_envy goto again
set met_envy 1
say The Watcher: Do you see them? Up there, on the terrace above. They climb so easily.
say The Watcher: Why them? Why always them, and never me?

label again
say The Watcher: I can't take my eyes off them. If I look away, they'll get even further ahead.
choice They had it easier than you. = easier | Then stop looking and climb. = stop | Someone below is watching you too. = right

label easier
say The Watcher: They did! They always did! You see it too!
end

label stop
say The Watcher: You don't understand. You've never had to watch.
end

label right
say The Watcher: ...Below me? Someone looks at me the way I look at them?
say The Watcher: All this time I've been counting their steps instead of taking my own.
say The Watcher: I don't want anyone to look at me like that. I'll climb.
set freed_envy 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The Watcher: I don't know who climbed faster. I stopped counting. It's so quiet without it.
end
