// The First Terrace: a sculptor who can't leave his statue of himself.

if freed_pride goto thanks
if met_pride goto again
set met_pride 1
say The Sculptor: Careful! Don't stand in front of it. Look at it. My finest work.
say The Sculptor: Every line of it is me, as I always knew I was. They will come to see it. They must.

label again
say The Sculptor: How can I climb before they come? Who would look at it then?
choice It's a masterpiece. Stay with it. = flatter | No one is coming to see it. = right | Then smash it. = smash

label flatter
say The Sculptor: Yes. Yes, you understand! I'll wait here a little longer.
end

label smash
say The Sculptor: Smash it? It's all I am! Get away from it!
end

label right
say The Sculptor: ...No one. In all this time, not one.
say The Sculptor: I spent my whole life carving the man I wanted them to see. I never once asked who I was without it.
say The Sculptor: I think I'd like to find out. Up there.
set freed_pride 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The Sculptor: I left the statue behind. I don't miss it. Isn't that strange?
end
