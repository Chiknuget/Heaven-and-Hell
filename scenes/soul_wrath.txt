// The Third Terrace: a man who won't stop shouting at people who aren't there.

if freed_wrath goto thanks
if met_wrath goto again
set met_wrath 1
say The Shouting Man: GET BACK! Every one of them who ever wronged me is still out there, and I will NOT forget a single one!

label again
say The Shouting Man: I remember every word they said. Every face. I hold on to all of it!
choice Who wronged you? = who | Are they here? Or is it only you? = right | Then shout louder. = louder

label who
say The Shouting Man: Who DIDN'T? My brother, my landlord, the man at the market, the...
say The Shouting Man: No, I have to start again. I can't forget any of them!
end

label louder
say The Shouting Man: Don't mock me! You're just like them!
end

label right
say The Shouting Man: ...Only me.
say The Shouting Man: I've been shouting at people who aren't here. For how long?
say The Shouting Man: I'm tired. I'm so tired of being angry. Let me put it down.
set freed_wrath 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The Shouting Man: It's strange to hear my own voice so quiet. I'd forgotten what it sounded like.
end
