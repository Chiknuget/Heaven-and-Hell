// Old Hesk the gravedigger: the second clue. It drags its victims to the Caves.

if tormentor_dead goto after
if climb_done goto mission
say Old Hesk: Busy year for a gravedigger. Don't make it busier. Get indoors.
end

label mission
if clue_grave goto again
set clue_grave 1
add clues 1
say Old Hesk: It doesn't kill like a wolf. A wolf leaves something behind. This takes them whole.
say Old Hesk: Drags them off to the hole by the road, the one that goes down into the earth. I've followed the marks to the edge. Never followed them in.
say Old Hesk: ...Hold on. Let me look at you.
say Old Hesk: Not long ago it went for someone on that road. Then it just stopped. Turned around and went back into the dark. Never seen it do that before.
say {name}: ...
say Old Hesk: Never mind. Old eyes, long year. Go on.
end

label again
say Old Hesk: The hole by the road. Follow the marks, if you're braver than me.
end

label after
say Old Hesk: First week in a year I haven't dug a single grave. I don't know what to do with my hands.
end
