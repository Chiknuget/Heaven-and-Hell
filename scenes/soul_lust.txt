// The Seventh Terrace: a soul standing in the purifying fire, afraid to walk through.

if freed_lust goto thanks
if met_lust goto again
set met_lust 1
say The One in the Fire: Don't come closer. The fire is all that's left of me now.
say The One in the Fire: I chased every heart that smiled at me, and never stayed with any of them. Now I'm afraid to walk through.

label again
say The One in the Fire: What if the fire burns away everything I am?
choice Then stay where you are. = stay | Forget all of them. = forget | It only burns what isn't worth keeping. = right

label stay
say The One in the Fire: Yes. Stay. It's easier to stay.
end

label forget
say The One in the Fire: I can't! They're all I ever was!
end

label right
say The One in the Fire: ...What's worth keeping.
say The One in the Fire: Is there anything of me like that?
say The One in the Fire: There's only one way to find out.
set freed_lust 1
add freed 1
if freed < 7 goto done
objective climb All seven souls are free. Climb to the summit.
label done
end

label thanks
say The One in the Fire: The fire didn't hurt. It only took what I didn't need.
end
