// The prologue: waking in Heaven, what the traveler saw, and the judgment.
// 60 frames = 1 second.

wait 40
say {name}: Where... where am I?
say {name}: The last thing I remember is the road. Then nothing.
say {name}: This place is too quiet. Too bright.

// Let the player walk until they reach the spot between the two back pillars
explore 4 1

// What he saw. The player never sees it.
fade 255 255 255 255 6
say {name}: What... is that?
say {name}: No. No, I shouldn't have seen that.
fade 255 255 255 0 60
wait 30

// The light grows until it fills the screen
glow 400 180
say The Voice: Be still, {name}.
say The Voice: You were not called here. You walked in as no living soul ever has, and you have seen what was never meant for living eyes.
say The Voice: What has been seen cannot be unseen. You cannot return to the world of the living carrying it.
say {name}: Then what happens to me?
say The Voice: By rights, you belong to the deep places. Hell would keep you, and keep your silence.
say The Voice: But I will offer you another road.
say The Voice: There is work below that the dead cannot do and My angels will not. Souls lost on the mountain. A village crying out. One who escaped his judgment. And something old, sleeping at the bottom of the world.
say The Voice: Serve Me in these things, and you will be judged by what you do, not by what you saw.
say The Voice: Choose, {name}.
choice Serve = serve | Refuse = refuse

label refuse
say The Voice: So be it.
fade 140 0 0 255 60
fade 0 0 0 255 60
caption And so {name} went down into the dark, and the dark kept his silence forever.
menu

label serve
say The Voice: Then go down. The way will open before you.
say The Voice: And {name}... remember that you are still alive. Down there, that will matter more than you know.
glow 0 90
settile 5 8 v
banner
end
