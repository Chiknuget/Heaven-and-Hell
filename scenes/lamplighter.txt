// Tobias the Lamplighter: the first clue. It only comes in the dark.

if tormentor_dead goto after
if climb_done goto mission
say Tobias: Lamps need oil, oil needs coin, and coin needs daylight. Off you go, stranger.
end

label mission
if clue_lamp goto again
set clue_lamp 1
add clues 1
say Tobias: Careful, that one's hot. I keep every lamp in this village lit. Every hour of every day.
say Tobias: It only ever comes when it's dark. I've never once seen it step into the light. So I don't let it get dark.
say Tobias: Some nights I think these lamps are the only reason any of us are still here.
end

label again
say Tobias: Light, and more light. That's all I know.
end

label after
say Tobias: I let the lamp by the road go out last night, just to see. Nothing came. I still didn't sleep.
end
