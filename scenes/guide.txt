// The guide, met only on the traveler's first death. Starts the first mission.

if met_guide goto again
set met_guide 1

say The Guide: Peace, {name}. Do not be afraid.
say The Guide: You have died, but your journey is not over. This is Purgatory, the mountain where souls are made clean.
say {name}: Something killed me. In the dark. I never even saw it.
say The Guide: Whatever it was, it cannot follow you here. And you did not come here by chance. The Voice that sent you has work for you on this mountain.
say The Guide: Seven terraces rise above us, one for each of the seven deadly sins. On each, a soul has stopped climbing. They cling to what they were, and it holds them in place.
say The Guide: Help them let go. Each one you free will climb to the summit, and you will find them there.

mission climb The Climb
objective climb Free the souls on the seven terraces. Freed: {freed}/7

say The Guide: If you cannot wait, the toll gate will return you to the world at once. But nothing here is free. Those who take it carry a burden, and the burdened cannot enter Heaven.
say The Guide: Choose as you will. We will not meet again.
end

label again
say The Guide: Go, {name}. The mountain is waiting.
end
