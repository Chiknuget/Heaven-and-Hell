// Runs every time the summit is reached. Once all seven souls are free,
// the first mission ends here and the second begins.

if climb_done goto done
if freed < 7 goto done

wait 30
say {name}: They're all here. Every one of them.
say The Sculptor: We waited for you, {name}. We wanted you to see us like this.

glow 260 120
say The Voice: Well done, {name}. Seven souls who could not move are climbing again.
say The Voice: But the thing that took your life still walks the world above. A village on the Surface has learned to fear the dark because of it.
say The Voice: Go back. Find that village.
glow 0 90

complete climb
set climb_done 1
mission village The Village
objective village Somewhere on the Surface, a village cries out. Find it.

label done
end
